# 具身智能与强化学习科研实践

在南京大学人工智能学院 LAMDA 实验室进组科研，围绕 RL（离线强化学习、安全强化学习与多智能体）与 VLA 具身智能相关领域开展研究，主要关注智能体在复杂真实场景中的安全性与泛化能力。

#### 科研方向与兴趣

- 离线强化学习与安全强化学习
- 多智能体强化学习
- VLA

#### 主要工作

- 算法调研与改进：深入研读 Safe RL 与 Offline RL 前沿论文并进行组会汇报，探索生成模型、元学习等方法在强化学习任务泛化等场景的应用
- 具身智能探索：参与 VLA 相关文章调研并汇报，协助机器人遥操数据收集与仿真环境搭建

#### 论文在投

- **Conservatism Controllable Compositional Guidance for Offline Safe Reinforcement Learning**（NeurIPS 2026 在投）— 提出 Co3G 方法，在离线安全强化学习基准上显著优于硬约束与软约束 baselines

### **Reinforcement Learning and Embodied Intelligence Research Practice**

Research training at RL Lab (LAMDA), Nanjing University, focusing on safe decision-making and generalization in real-world embodied settings.

#### Key Research Directions and Interests

- Offline reinforcement learning and safe reinforcement learning
- Multi-agent reinforcement learning
- VLA

#### Key Work

- Studied and presented frontier Safe RL and Offline RL papers; explored generative models and meta-learning for RL task generalization.
- Explored VLA-related literature and supported teleoperation data collection and simulation setup.

#### Paper under review

- Conservatism Controllable Compositional Guidance for Offline Safe Reinforcement Learning (NeurIPS 2026) — proposed Co3G, outperforming hard- and soft-constraint baselines on offline safe RL benchmarks.
