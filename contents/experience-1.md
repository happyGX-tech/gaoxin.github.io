# Reinforcement Learning & Embodied Intelligence / 强化学习与具身智能科研实践

LAMDA Lab (RL Lab), School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University · 2025.11 — Present

Research training focusing on safe decision-making and generalization of agents in complex real-world embodied settings.

在南京大学人工智能学院 LAMDA 实验室 RL Lab 进组科研，主要关注智能体在复杂真实场景中的安全性与泛化能力。

## Research Directions / 研究方向

- Offline reinforcement learning & safe reinforcement learning / 离线强化学习与安全强化学习
- Multi-agent reinforcement learning / 多智能体强化学习
- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) embodied models / VLA 具身模型

## Key Work / 主要工作

- Studied and presented frontier Safe RL and Offline RL papers; explored generative models and meta-learning for RL task generalization.
- Built RL environments and reproduced multi-agent algorithm baselines; exploring generative models for multi-agent RL.
- Surveyed VLA literature; supported robot teleoperation data collection and simulation setup.

- 深入研读 Safe RL 与 Offline RL 前沿论文并进行组会汇报，探索生成模型、元学习等方法在强化学习任务泛化中的应用。
- 搭建强化学习环境，复现并比较多智能体算法基线，探索生成式模型在多智能体强化学习中的应用。
- 调研 VLA 相关文献并汇报，协助机器人遥操数据收集与仿真环境搭建。

## Publication / 论文发表

  Ruiqi Xue, Shenghe Hu, **Xin Gao**, Jing-Wen Yang
