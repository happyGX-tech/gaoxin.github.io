My research centers on reinforcement learning and embodied intelligence — building embodied agents that can evolve on their own. At the LAMDA Lab of Nanjing University, I work on offline & safe RL, multi-agent decision-making, and vision-language-action models, toward agents that keep learning, adapt autonomously, and act safely in the real world.

我的研究兴趣主要集中在强化学习与具身智能，致力于构建能够自我进化的具身智能体。目前在南京大学 LAMDA 实验室开展研究，关注离线与安全强化学习、多智能体决策以及视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型，希望让智能体在真实世界中持续学习、自主适应并安全地交互。
